A reading tool, not a verdict

Paste a headline. See how it reads to a model trained on real and fake news.

Verascope looks at word patterns common to fabricated stories — sensational claims, vague sourcing, urgency — and compares them against patterns typical of reported news. It gives you a second opinion, not the final word.

    What happens when you click Analyze

    1. 1

      Text is cleaned

      URLs, HTML, punctuation and numbers are stripped so the model sees only meaningful words.

    2. 2

      Words become numbers

      A TF-IDF vectorizer scores each word by how distinctive it is to this piece of text.

    3. 3

      The model decides

      A Logistic Regression classifier, trained on labeled real and fake examples, returns a label and confidence.
